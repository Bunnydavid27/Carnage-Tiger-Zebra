V3 · PICK YOUR CHALLENGE

Two ways to play

NORMAL · SURVIVAL · MULTIPLAYER
CLASSIC CHASE

Normal

Race across the whole arena. Tiger can chase Zebra anywhere; Zebra bots route around arena obstacles to reach frozen teammates. Choose the Zebra team size (2–6 total players) before the round.

🐅 Tigerhunts anywhere🦓 Zebra
TERRITORY MODE

Survival

Zebra starts on small forest groves spread across Forest Land; their number follows the Zebra team size. Each grove protects one Zebra player, frozen or active. Tiger does not count toward grove capacity. If a second Zebra player enters the same grove, protection ends and Tiger can hunt them there. If Tiger freezes multiple Zebra players on one grove together, they are moved off the grove onto Forest Land while still frozen. If a Zebra meter has 70%, one of that group self-revives automatically after 3.5 seconds; the 70% cost and six-second rescue lock still apply. Players caught in a grove cannot be rescued for 2.5 seconds. Only one leaf exists at a time, away from groves. A new one replaces it every 30 seconds.

FOREST LANDmost of the arena · Tiger territory
SMALL ZEBRA GROVES · ONE SAFE PLAYER EACH
Zebra strategy: spread out, collect the leaf, rescue teammates, and return to a grove without crowding it.

Carry each leaf back to a grove to bank it. The eighth appears at 3:30 and vanishes at 3:45 if uncollected. If fewer than six are banked then, Tiger wins; if collected, bank it by 4:00.

Zebra victory: bank at least six of the eight leaves and finish with every Zebra teammate free. Missed waves are replaced, but still count against the eight total chances. If the final leaf expires at 3:45 before six are banked, Tiger wins immediately. If two Zebra players share a grove, Tiger can enter and hunt both there.

THE ROUND, AT A GLANCE

Chase. Freeze. Rescue. Win.

01 · MOVE

Find your opening

Use WASD or arrow keys. On a phone, drag across the arena to steer.

02 · FREEZE

Tiger tags Zebra

As Tiger, get close to an active Zebra player to freeze them. Keep moving—teammates can rescue them after 0.9 seconds frozen on Forest Land or 2.5 seconds if caught in a grove.

03 · RESCUE

Zebra frees a teammate

Touch a frozen teammate to rescue them after the wait: 0.9 seconds on Forest Land, 2.5 seconds if they were caught in a grove. If Tiger catches multiple Zebra players on one grove at once, the frozen group is moved onto Forest Land; one eligible 70% meter triggers a self-revive after 3.5 seconds. A Zebra player who is blinking after revival cannot rescue anyone until the six-second shield ends. If you are frozen with 70% energy, use Power to break free; if all Zebra are frozen and nobody has 70%, Tiger wins immediately.

04 · POWER UP

Separate team meters

Both meters start at 0%. Tiger gains 30% for each freeze and can spend 30% for a 40% speed boost lasting 2.8 seconds. Zebra gains 50% per rescue and 10% per leaf; a freeze drains 5%. Zebra powers cost 50%, or 70% to break free while frozen. Rescued or self-freed Zebra gets six seconds of protection. In bot modes, your Zebra character can use the team self-revive. In Multiplayer, a full-team freeze allows one self-revive: among players with 70%, the earliest frozen goes first (highest score breaks ties). The limit resets when all Zebra players are free. No eligible 70% meter means Tiger wins.

HOW TO WIN

One round. Two ways to victory.

🐅
TIGER WINS

Freeze all Zebra players. Tiger wins immediately if everyone is frozen and no frozen Zebra player has 70% energy to break free. At time-up in Normal, Tiger wins if at least two Zebra players are still frozen. In Survival and Multiplayer, any frozen Zebra player means Tiger wins.

🦓
ZEBRA WINS

Reach the timer with every teammate free. In Survival, also deliver the required drops.

🏆

Player scorecard: Tiger players rank on the left; Zebra players rank on the right, each high to low. Tiger earns 1 point per freeze; Zebra earns 1 per leaf and 3 per teammate rescued. This score is separate from the team win.

NEARBY PLAY · THREE QUICK STEPS

Host on your laptop

01
💻▶
START THE SERVER

On Windows, open Run-Game.cmd. Keep the laptop and server running.

02
🎮🔗
CREATE & SHARE

Choose Multiplayer → Room → Create Private Room. Set an optional passcode, then tap COPY LINK. Share the passcode separately. Players choose teams: up to two Tiger, up to six Zebra. The room needs at least two humans, one Tiger and one Zebra. Choose 2–8 total characters in the lobby; bots fill the rest. It auto-starts when everyone is ready, or the host can press START MATCH once both teams are present.

03
📱✓
OPEN & JOIN

Connect phones to the laptop hotspot or same Wi-Fi. Open the invite link, enter the passcode if the host set one, then press CONNECT TO ROOM.

⚡

Quick Match queue: Everyone opens the same server address and taps FIND A QUICK MATCH. Players share a lobby of up to 8 (2 Tiger, 6 Zebra). At least 2 players are needed: 1 Tiger and 1 Zebra. The match auto-starts when everyone is ready, or the host can click START MATCH once both teams are present. Across the Internet, use the shared HTTPS tunnel or a deployed public server.

🔐

Private room: Set an optional passcode when creating, then share it separately from the invite. Guests enter it before connecting. Use HTTPS when playing online.

📡

Hotspot tip: Turn on the hotspot from the laptop and connect phones to it. If the laptop is connected to a phone’s hotspot, that phone may block incoming connections. Allow Node.js through Windows Firewall on private networks if prompted.

LONG-DISTANCE PLAY · INTERNET ROOM

Host an Internet room

Start Run-Game.cmd, then Start-Remote-Tunnel.cmd. Everyone opens the same printed HTTPS address. Choose Find a Quick Match to join the shared queue, or create a private room and share its link (and passcode separately). Keep both host processes running. The temporary tunnel works across networks and devices; for always-on matchmaking, deploy the server on a public Node.js host.

Quick Tunnels are temporary and for testing. For longer sessions, use a named Cloudflare Tunnel or a hosted room server.
